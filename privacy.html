Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Welwise Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you play Relaxing Mini Games (the "Game") on Facebook and Facebook Gaming.

The Game is developed and operated by Welwise Studio, which is the data controller for the information described below. You can reach us at welwisestudio@gmail.com.

By playing the Game you accept this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not play the Game.

Information we collect

The Game uses the Facebook SDK for Unity, provided by Meta Platforms. The Game does not use Facebook Login and does not request access to your Facebook profile. We do not ask you to create a separate account. The Game has no in-game purchases, so we never receive payment details.

Category What it includes How we get it
Identifiers An app-specific or advertising identifier assigned by Meta or your device. It does not include your name Collected automatically by the Facebook SDK
Device and technical data Device model, operating system, browser type, language, time zone, IP address, screen size Collected automatically by the Facebook SDK
Usage data App launches, session length and in-game events such as levels started and completed Collected automatically by the Facebook SDK
Game progress Scores, unlocked content and settings Created by the Game as you play

We do not request your name, profile picture, email address, phone number, friends list, precise location, contacts or payment data.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run and improve the Game:

  • to let you launch the Game and save your progress;
  • to understand how the Game is played, fix bugs and improve gameplay;
  • to keep the Game secure and prevent cheating or abuse;
  • to meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are: performance of our Terms of Service with you, our legitimate interest in operating and improving the Game, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

We share information only with the parties below.

  • Meta Platforms. The Game runs on Facebook and uses the Facebook SDK, so Meta receives the identifier, device and usage data described above. Meta handles it under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising. The Game may show ads served by Meta. Meta may use the data it collects to select and measure those ads. We do not pass your data to any other advertising network.
  • Service providers. Companies that host or maintain the Game for us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities and successors. When the law requires it, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of the Game.

Retention

We keep information linked to your identifier for as long as you play the Game. We delete or anonymise it within 30 days after you send us a deletion request. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be kept longer.

To use any of these rights, email welwisestudio@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Step-by-step instructions are on our Data Deletion Instructions page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
