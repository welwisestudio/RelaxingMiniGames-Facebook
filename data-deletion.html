Data Deletion Instructions

Relaxing Mini Games (the "Game") is developed and operated by Welwise Studio. You can ask us to delete any data the Game holds about you at any time. Deletion is free.

What data the Game holds

The Game does not use Facebook Login. It does not receive your name, profile picture, email address, friends list or payment data. It holds only your game progress and the technical and usage data collected through the Facebook SDK, linked to an identifier and not to your name.

Step 1. Send us a deletion request

Email welwisestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request: Relaxing Mini Games". Include the device and platform you played on. We check what we hold, and if nothing is linked to you, we tell you so.

We confirm your request within 7 days and delete your data within 30 days. We email you when the deletion is complete.

Step 2. Remove the Game from your Facebook account

This stops the Game from receiving any further data from Facebook. If the Game is not listed there, there is no connection to remove.

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy, then Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites.
  3. Find Relaxing Mini Games and select Remove.
  4. Confirm by selecting Remove again.

What we delete

We delete all data linked to your identifier: game progress and usage records. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be kept. We keep other records only where the law requires it.

Data held by Meta

Meta collects and stores some data on its own behalf when you use Facebook. We cannot delete that data. You can manage it in your Facebook settings and read about it in Meta's Privacy Policy.
